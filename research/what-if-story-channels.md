# What If story channels: research vs. the 2D Video plugin

Research date: 2026-11-07. Data from Nexlev and vidIQ (channel stats, outliers, and full AI watch-throughs of 4 top videos).

## TL;DR

You're right. The 2D Video plugin is built for a **moody cinematic explainer** (black stick figures, rim light, letterbox, dark low-key sets, blueprint science segment, one narrator). The What If channels blowing up right now are making **POV sitcom/drama stories**: "you" narration, a named cast with voiced dialogue, bright daytime palettes, a persistent on-screen counter tied to the premise, phone/chat UI everywhere, and a twist ending. The plugin's look and story template fit the *science* What If format (xkcd style), not the *story* format.

## Two different What If formats

| | A. Science What If | B. Story What If (the one growing fast) |
|---|---|---|
| Example title | What if the Moon disappeared? | What if you were reborn with all your memories? |
| Subject | The world, physics, space | **You**, your life, your crush, your family |
| Narration | Explainer voice | Second person ("You wake up and...") + character dialogue |
| Payoff | "Is it real?" science | Emotional resolution + twist |
| Examples | xkcd's What If? (1.38M subs), What would happen? (3D) | OTHERWISE, Nick ifverse, Infinite Ifs, One Small If, What If Animations |
| Plugin fit | Good | **Poor** |

## What the top story videos actually do (4 watched end to end)

Videos: OTHERWISE "Reborn With All Your Memories", Nick ifverse "Love Meter", One Small If "Doubled in Size Every Hour", Infinite Ifs "Swapped Bodies With Your Crush".

### 1. Narration: "you" + a voiced cast
- 100% second person: "You're 82...", "You wake up, walk to the mirror, and your crush Emma is staring back at you."
- **Every video has a named supporting cast with voiced dialogue**: Dad, best friend (Rocky / Theo / Mike), the crush (Emma), a snarky sister, a remote scientist on speakerphone, a cynical uncle, a rival. 3 to 6 side characters.
- Narrator tone: deadpan, warm, slightly funny. Dialogue is short and punchy.

### 2. Characters
- Bean / round heads, stick or capsule limbs. Close to the plugin's figures, **but**:
- Real outfits, not one accent item: hoodie + sneakers + headphones, pink polka-dot pajamas, flat cap + glasses, suit + tie + sunglasses, backward cap, hair tufts, ponytails.
- Big expressive eyes (one channel uses anime eyes for the love interest), brows, **blush and sweat-drop icons**, comic reactions.
- One Small If is the closest to the plugin look (black heads with a coloured rim highlight, built on a custom code engine) and still does everything else on this list.

### 3. Colour and light
- **Bright, warm daytime palettes for normal life**: creams, pastel blues, suburban greens, warm yellows, autumn oranges.
- Dark navy/indigo only for crisis beats: deathbed, night, the low point.
- Palette shift follows the emotional arc (day -> golden hour -> night).
- No letterbox, no constant film-noir lighting.

### 4. A persistent HUD tied to the premise (the biggest signature)
Every video keeps the premise's core variable on screen the whole time:
- Doubled in size: top-left **clock + height** (6:00 AM / 5 FT 7 IN -> 7:30 PM / 12 MI) plus a side altitude ladder (house, school, skyscraper, clouds, planes, Everest).
- Reborn: top-left **year + age** pill, top-right **NET WORTH** counter ($20,000 -> $16.2M -> "A LOT"), a missed-calls counter that ends at zero.
- Body swap: top-right **"SWAP PERMANENT IN"** live countdown.
- Love meter: **floating score bubbles above every head** (5, 64, 100) that pop when they change.

### 5. In-universe UI everywhere
Phone text threads, video-call screens, game HUD + chat box, GPS routes, AI search box, browser dialogs, stock/crypto charts, notebook checklists, RPG-style **character intro banners**, time cards, comic text pops ("MOM!", "GET OUT!"), split screens.

### 6. Story structure (all four follow it)
1. **Cold open in media res** (0-15 s): the most extreme moment first (giant catching a jet, dying alone at 82, waking up in your crush's body).
2. **Rewind** effect (VHS rewind, iris wipe, "life rewind pull") back to the start.
3. The premise line: "What if you...?"
4. **Fun/greed phase**: the power is cool, comedic attempts (3 failed tries at winning the crush, getting rich on Bitcoin).
5. **Cost and emotional core** revealed midway: save Dad from a 2018 heart attack, Emma's unsupportive mother, the square-cube law hurting.
6. Climax and heartfelt resolution (honesty, family).
7. **Twist / sequel bait in the last 10-20 s**: best friend swapped with the principal; the crush's mom was your gamer buddy; growth hormone spikes in sleep, eyes close.

No "is any of this real?" science segment, no end-screen lecture.

### 7. Pacing and sound
- 1.5-4 s per shot, puppet/cutout rig motion, zooms, pans, whooshes, split screens.
- Music: upbeat lo-fi / light pop / synth groove, dropping to soft piano for the emotional beats.
- Heavy comedic SFX: UI pops on every counter change, bonks, splashes, phone buzzes, ticking clocks, cash registers, monitor beeps.

## Where the 2D Video plugin is wrong for this

Note: your local studio (`C:\Users\beyon\Documents\5 YT - Consilyo Channel\2d video\2d-video\`) already has the custom **Clay & Sage** look (`clay`, made 2026-11-07): beige figures with black contour, hair styles bob/long/bun, trousers on everyone, no letterbox in 9:16, a varied cast via `vary()`. That fixes the "black stick people" and outfit rows below. The installed plugin itself is still stock (v0.1.1), so these rows describe the stock plugin. Clay still keeps the stock lighting rules (one visible key light, moody value structure) and the stock story template.

| Plugin rule (style-bible.md) | What story channels do | Clay & Sage |
|---|---|---|
| Low-key, moody, cinematic lighting in every shot, 60-75% dark frame | Bright daytime by default, dark only for low points | Still moody (teal-slate shadows, apricot key), needs a bright day mood |
| Pure black figures, ONE coloured garment | Full outfits, hair, hats, glasses, blush, sweat drops | Fixed: beige outlined figures, hair, trousers. Still missing blush/sweat drops, glasses |
| Letterbox bars by default | Full frame, HUD in the corners | Fixed for 9:16 |

The rows below apply to both stock and Clay:

| Plugin rule (style-bible.md) | What story channels do |
|---|---|
| One voiceover, "narrator is never lip-synced", talking only on quoted lines | Narrator + 3-6 voiced characters with constant dialogue |
| "Never rewrite the narration", script is VO-first | Story must be *written* for this format (cast, attempts, twist) |
| Data cards / badges occasionally, "keep text minimal" | Persistent premise HUD all video + phone/chat UI everywhere |
| Story ends with blueprint "Is any of this real?" segment + end screen | Ends on an emotional resolution and a twist |
| Freeze-frames, 3D orbits, blueprints as signature shots | Rewinds, split screens, phone screens, character intro banners as signature shots |

What the plugin gets right and should keep: code-drawn consistent characters, crisp text, shots synced to word timestamps, 2-4 s cuts, generated music/SFX with ducking.

## Recommendation

Add a **"story" mode** to the pipeline instead of forcing stories through the explainer look:

1. **New style preset `daylight`** (warm pastel day, golden hour, night variants) with no letterbox, lighter grade, optional rim light.
2. **Character upgrade**: outfit layers (top, bottom, shoes), hair shapes, hats, glasses, big-eye variant, blush and sweat-drop overlays.
3. **Premise HUD component**: persistent corner counters (clock, age/year, money, countdown, meter), plus floating above-head meters. Value keyframes come from `shots.json`.
4. **UI kit**: phone text thread, video call, game chat, browser/search box, checklist, character intro banner, time card, comic text pop, split screen, rewind transition.
5. **Multi-voice dialogue**: accept a script with speaker labels (`NARRATOR:`, `DAD:`, `EMMA:`), generate or accept one voice per character, lip-sync characters on their own lines.
6. **Story template** in `story-breakdown.md`: cold open -> rewind -> premise -> fun phase (3 attempts) -> cost / emotional core -> climax -> twist. Drop the blueprint science segment for this mode.
7. **Script writing step** before VO: the plugin currently assumes the VO exists; story channels live or die on the script (cast, stakes, twist).

Title formula that's working: **"What If You [personal power or situation] [+ a catch]?"** with stakes in love, money, family, or your own body.
